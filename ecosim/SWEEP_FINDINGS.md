# Sweep findings

These results come from the six required sweeps, plus one extra variant of sweep 5. Every cell ran seeds 1, 2 and 3 for 20000 ticks with `--jobs 8`. Each cell was evaluated with the same invariants as `ecosim check`, except the runtime invariant. **Nothing was tuned in response to these findings.** `params.toml` is unchanged.

Each sweep's full tables are in its `sweeps/<name>/sweep.md`, and the per-cell values and margins are in `sweep.csv`. The per-tick series are regenerated by rerunning the sweep and are not committed. Definitions (from `DECISIONS.md` → Sweep harness):

- A value is **safe** when all of its cells pass.
- The **band** is the contiguous run of safe values that contains the default.
- A band is **fragile** when it has fewer than 3 grid values, i.e. is narrower than two steps.
- The **first failing** invariant at an edge is the one failing in the most cells at the neighbouring value.

## Headline

1. **Hunter extinction is the dominant failure mode.** Sweeps 1–4 and 6 have 38 failing cells, and 29 of them fail `no_extinction`.
   - In 23 of those 29, hunters die first, usually in the second half of the run (ticks 9000–18800).
   - In the other 6, grazers die first: this is refugium ≥ 0.7, where the grazers are hunted out and the hunters follow.
   - The remaining 9 failing cells are `tree.mature_age` edge effects: 8 `max_10x` failures and 1 `grass_band` failure.
2. **Four of the five swept parameters sit in a fragile or noisy band.** `refugium_shrub`, `mature_age` and `season.amplitude` each pass at the default grid value only. In sweep 6, `kill_prob` has no value at which all 9 cells pass. Only `kill_prob` alone gives a band of three values.
3. **The grazer cycle does not vanish at `season.amplitude=0`.** By the stated criterion the answer is YES on all three seeds (details below), and the large crash-and-recovery oscillation persists without any seasonal forcing. The cycle is weaker, though, and the runs fail other invariants.

## Per parameter

### 1. `grazer.energy_cost` (0.04:0.16:0.02, default 0.08)

- **Safe band: [0.04, 0.08]**, 3 of 7 values. **Not fragile** by the rule.
- Lower edge: passes to the end of the grid (0.04).
- Upper edge: at 0.10, the first failing invariant is `no_extinction` on seed 1 (hunters extinct at tick 15315).
- Past the edge the failures are sporadic rather than monotone:
  - 0.12 and 0.16 pass on all seeds.
  - 0.14 fails on seed 3 only (hunters extinct at 16742).
  - Each failure is one late hunter extinction on one seed. That points to seed noise near a knife edge, not a clean threshold.
- Grazer peak population falls steadily with cost, from about 8500 at 0.04 to about 3500 at 0.16. No other invariant comes close to failing.

### 2. `hunter.kill_prob` (0.1:0.5:0.1, default 0.2)

- **Safe band: [0.2, 0.4]**, 3 of 5 values. **Not fragile**, but the default sits on the lower edge.
- Lower edge: at 0.1, the first failing invariant is `no_extinction` on seeds 1, 2 and 3.
  - Hunters starve at ticks 14235, 9272 and 13325, peaking at only 12–20 animals.
  - `animals_10k` also fails there on seeds 2 and 3, with 0 and 1 hunters at tick 10000.
- Upper edge: at 0.5, the first failing invariant is `no_extinction` on seed 2. Hunters overkill: they die out at 12602 and grazers follow at 13383.
- Grazers at tick 10000 fall steeply with kill_prob. At 0.4 there are only 73–129 grazers, against the `animals_10k` floor of 10, so the upper edge is close.

### 3. `hunter.refugium_shrub` (0.2:0.8:0.1, default 0.58)

- **Safe band: [0.6, 0.6]**, 1 of 7 values. **Fragile.**
  - The default 0.58 is not a grid value, so 0.6 is the nearest grid point, and it is the only one where all 3 seeds pass.
- Lower edge: at 0.5, the first failing invariant is `no_extinction` on seeds 1, 2 and 3.
  - Too many grazers are protected, so hunters starve (extinct at 17317, 16067 and 14272).
  - Every value from 0.2 to 0.5 fails this way, earlier the lower the threshold (about 9600–13300 at 0.2–0.4).
- Upper edge: at 0.7, the first failing invariant is `no_extinction` on seeds 1, 2 and 3.
  - Too few refugia: grazers are hunted out (extinct at 9577, 10999 and 10041), and hunters follow about 1200 ticks later.
  - `grazer_cycle` (seeds 1 and 3) and `animals_10k` (seeds 1 and 3) also fail.
  - 0.8 behaves the same.
- This is the most sensitive knob in the model. The stable region is narrower than one grid step of 0.1.

### 4. `tree.mature_age` (500:2500:500, default 1000)

- **Safe band: [1000, 1000]**, 1 of 5 values. **Fragile.**
- Lower edge: at 500, the first failing invariant is `no_extinction` on seed 3 (hunters extinct at 15061). `grass_band` also fails on seed 1, where the grass mean is 0.0448, below the 0.05 floor, because the canopy closes early.
- Upper edge: at 1500, the first failing invariant is `max_10x` on seeds 1, 2 and 3.
  - It is trees that exceed 10× their tick-2000 count: peaks of 613–658 against limits of about 480–530.
  - Slower maturation keeps the tick-2000 tree count low, so the ratio blows up. The margin falls from −0.28 at 1500 to −0.65 at 2000 to about −4 at 2500.
  - This is partly an artefact of anchoring `max_10x` at tick 2000 for a slow-growing species. Across all five values, the tree counts peak within a similar 570–660 range.

### 5. `season.amplitude` (0:12:3, default 12)

- **Safe band: [12, 12]**, 1 of 5 values. **Fragile.**
- Lower edge: at 9, the first failing invariant is `no_extinction` on seed 2 (hunters extinct at 12567). `grass_band` also fails on seed 3, but only barely (margin −0.006).
- Upper edge: passes to the end of the grid; 12 is the maximum swept.
- Amplitudes 3, 6 and 9 each pass on 1 of 3 seeds, and 0 passes on none. Failures mix the following:
  - `no_extinction` (hunters).
  - `grass_band` (grass mean below 0.05).
  - `fertility_band`. It fails on all seeds at amplitude 0, where mean fertility is 222–226, just above the upper bound. A likely cause, not verified here, is that without a warm-season growth pulse less fertility is drawn down.
- The seasonal swing apparently props up both the grass mean and hunter persistence.
- The default amplitude=12 cells reproduce the baseline runs exactly: grazer max 5939, 6110 and 6010 on seeds 1, 2 and 3, matching `ecosim run`. So renaming `climate.temp_amp` to `season.amplitude` changed nothing.

## The season-amplitude question

> At `season.amplitude=0`, does the grazer series still have ≥ 2 local maxima after tick 4000 with peak-to-trough ratio ≥ 1.5, on all three seeds?

**Answer: YES, on all three seeds.** The numbers are from sweep 5, `sweeps/season_amplitude`.

Method (`sweeps/cycle_ratio.py`):

- The script uses the same 200-tick centred moving average and ±500-tick local-maximum rule as `check.rs`. It cross-checks its peak count from tick 2000 against `sweep.csv`'s `grazer_peaks`.
- Each maximum after tick 4000 is paired with the minimum of the smoothed series between the previous maximum (or tick 4000) and itself.
- ratio = smoothed peak / smoothed trough.

The notation below is `peak tick: peak / trough @ trough tick = ratio`. Bold ratios are ≥ 1.5, and † marks a peak after hunter extinction.

| seed | maxima after 4000 | qualifying (≥ 1.5) | per-peak ratios | hunters extinct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | **3** | 5243: 5152/3327@4000 = **1.55**; 7696: 1.23; 13104: 1173/97@9876 = **12.13**; 14953: 1.05; 18855: 462/293@17683 = **1.58**† | 18818 |
| 2 | 6 | **3** | 5621: 6416/3697@4000 = **1.74**; 8421: 1.10; 9535: 1.05; 13604: 1626/355@10294 = **4.59**; 15577: 1.25; 19901: 765/453@18078 = **1.69** | never |
| 3 | 5 | **2** | 5853: 1.49; 9325: 1.06; 13511: 1590/131@10605 = **12.12**; 17957: 720/344@15816 = **2.09**†; 18893: 1.07† | 14458 |

Per-seed ratios of the qualifying peaks:

- seed 1: 1.55, 12.13, 1.58
- seed 2: 1.74, 4.59, 1.69
- seed 3: 12.12, 2.09

For comparison, at the default amplitude of 12 there are 4 qualifying peaks on every seed, and hunters survive on all three:

- s1: 1.99, 3.36, 10.64, 2.15
- s2: 1.61, 1.81, 2.00, 7.29
- s3: 1.53, 1.66, 1.51, 1.67

Caveats that bear on shot 4:

- **The criterion passes, but narrowly.** Seed 3 has exactly 2 qualifying peaks.
- **A stricter reading fails on seeds 1 and 3.** Under that reading, a peak counts only if its trough lies after tick 4000 rather than being clipped to it, and only if hunters are alive at the peak.
  - Seed 1: 1 qualifying peak (12.13). Its 1.55 is the tail of the initial boom, and its 1.58 comes after hunters die.
  - Seed 2: 2 qualifying peaks (4.59, 1.69).
  - Seed 3: 1 qualifying peak (12.12).
- **One large oscillation survives on every seed without seasons.** Grazers crash to about 100–350 around ticks 9900–10600 and recover roughly 5–13× by about 13100–13600. That is the predator–prey cycle, and it does not need seasonal forcing.
  - What is lost without seasons is the regular smaller oscillations that seasons pace. It becomes one big cycle followed by drift, and on 2 of 3 seeds hunter extinction late in the run.
- **The amplitude-0 runs fail `ecosim check`.**
  - Seeds 1 and 3 fail `no_extinction`.
  - All three seeds fail `fertility_band`.
  - Seeds 1 and 2 fail `grass_band`.
  - So "the cycle persists" does not mean "amplitude 0 is a viable configuration".

**Extra variant: no seasonal rain either** (`--set climate.rain_amp=0`, `sweeps/season_amplitude_no_rain_season`). `season.amplitude` controls temperature only, and rain has its own seasonal term (`climate.rain_amp`, 4). With both at 0 there is no seasonal forcing at all, and the answer is still **YES** on all three seeds:

| seed | qualifying ratios | notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.55, 12.13, 1.58 | hunters, trees, grass and shrub are identical to sweep 5; grazers differ on 9 ticks |
| 2 | 1.74, 4.59, 1.69 | series identical to sweep 5 apart from moisture and fertility |
| 3 | 4.24, 12.00†, 7.51† | hunters extinct at 16878; the last two peaks are grazers recovering from 2 and 10 animals |

On seeds 1 and 2, turning the rain season off at temperature amplitude 0 changes only the moisture and fertility columns (plus 9 grazer ticks on seed 1). The populations are unaffected, so the rain season is a negligible driver next to the temperature season.

Note that with the rain season off, **no amplitude passes at all**, including the default 12, which fails `grass_band` on all seeds. The current calibration depends on both seasonal terms.

**Implication for shot 4:** the cycle does not vanish at amplitude 0. So shot 4 does not have to rework hunter dynamics from scratch to get a cycle. It does, however, have to deal with the dominant failure mode that every sweep exposes: slow hunter extinction in the second half of the run, with a stability region narrower than one grid step in `refugium_shrub`.
