# Collapse atlas (shot 14)

Three two-parameter sweeps, each on seeds 1–3 at 20 000 ticks, with every other parameter at its default. This is a report only: nothing was tuned on these results, and no rule, default or format changed. The commands and wall times are in `_log.txt`. The full invariant tables are in `<grid>/sweep.md` and `sweep.csv`. `atlas.py` reads the per-cell series, which are gitignored and regenerated by rerunning the logged commands, and prints the grids and noise tables below.

**How to read a grid**
- Each cell shows seeds 1, 2 and 3 left to right: 🟩 persist, 🟨 grazer collapse, 🟥 hunter collapse, ⬛ both.
- A species has collapsed when its count reaches 0 at any tick. With every immigration floor at 0, it never comes back.
- After the glyphs is the most common first collapse among the cell's seeds, with its dominant cause over the 500 ticks ending at the extinction tick (the `ecosim stats` rule). G is grazers, H is hunters.
- † marks a cell in which trees reach 0 on some seed. Trees aren't one of the four classes.
- Collapse and failing `ecosim check` aren't the same thing. Many cells fail only `fertility_band`, `mature_trees_10k` or `animals_10k` with both animal species alive. `sweep.md` lists those failures.

**Flips and noise**
- A cell *flips* when its three seeds don't all have the same outcome.
- Every flipping cell was rerun on `rng.stream` 1–5 for each seed, which makes 18 replicates per cell with stream 0 included.
- The stream switches after terrain generation, so a seed keeps its world and each stream replays new dynamics on it (`DECISIONS.md`).
- A flip is *seed-determined* when each seed repeats its stream-0 outcome on all five extra streams, so the world decides. Otherwise it is *noise*.

**Summary: all 19 flips are noise, 0 are seed-determined**
- **rain_x_kill** has 11 flips, all noise. It is the only grid with real structure: a persist band in the middle, hunters starving at low `kill_prob`, and both species collapsing at high `kill_prob` × high `rain_amp`. Its flips lie on the borders between those regions.
- **energy_x_fire** has 4 flips, all noise.
- **crowd_x_disease** has 4 flips, all noise.
- In those last two grids every stream-0 collapse is on seed 3, and the cells are scattered rather than forming a region. On the stream reruns the same cells collapse in only 1–3 of 18 replicates, on seeds 2 and 3.
- **What this means for the anchor.** No seed's world is intrinsically fragile inside the persist region. Seed 3 at stream 0 is one unlucky draw of the dynamics, and the three anchor seeds sample the collapse probability rather than three fixed ecosystems.

**Regions**
- **Persist.** The persist cells form one contiguous region of 23, plus one isolated cell. The region is a band that slants toward lower `kill_prob` as `rain_amp` rises. It holds `kill_prob` 0.2 at every `rain_amp`, 0.3 up to `rain_amp` 5, 0.4 up to 2, and 0.1 from 4 up. The default, (4, 0.3), sits inside it.
- **Hunter collapse.** At `kill_prob` 0.1 with `rain_amp` ≤ 3, hunters starve out between ticks 15 000 and 19 400, while grazers persist. The unanimous cells, `rain_amp` 0–1, form a region of 2.
- **Both collapse.** At high `kill_prob` × high `rain_amp`, grazers are eaten out, mostly between ticks 7 000 and 13 000, and the hunters starve after them. The unanimous cells form one region of 8 (`rain_amp` 5–8 × `kill_prob` 0.4–0.5).
- **One grazer-only collapse.** At (6, 0.3) seed 2, the grazers are eaten out and the last hunters are still alive at tick 20 000.
- **The isolated persist cell** is (4, 0.5). It sits between two flipping cells and the both-collapse region, and it is where you'd expect a noisy edge.

**Flips**
- **All 11 flips lie on region borders.** The hunter-starvation edge is `kill_prob` 0.1 at `rain_amp` 2–3. The predation edge is `kill_prob` 0.3 at `rain_amp` 6–8, and 0.4–0.5 at `rain_amp` 0–4.
- **The border is a probability gradient.** The replicate collapse counts rise from 2/18 at (4, 0.4) to 12–14/18 at `kill_prob` 0.3 with `rain_amp` 6–8. There is no sharp line.
- **All 11 flips are noise.** Every one has at least one seed whose own world gives different outcomes on different streams.

**Rain amplitude has almost no effect below 3**
- At `rain_amp` 0 and 1 the grazer, hunter and tree series are byte-identical on every seed. Only `moisture_mean`, `fertility_mean` and `detritus_total` differ, so rows 0 and 1 of the grid are the same. This was checked on all 15 cell-seeds, comparing counts and death causes.
- At `rain_amp` 2, `kill_prob` 0.5 repeats the same 18 stream outcomes as rows 0 and 1.
- A seasonal rain swing this small leaves the soil wet enough that the producers never feel it.

**Trees**
- Trees die out on seed 2 only, at `rain_amp` 6–8, between ticks 1 350 and 1 600. That is before any animal collapses.
- The likely mechanism, untested: rain is lowest at tick 1 000 (a quarter year), where it is `rain_base − rain_amp`, close to 0 at these amplitudes. Trees that stay dry for `tree.dry_death_ticks` (500) die, and seed 2's terrain seems to have no wet refuge for them.
- The animals persist in some of these cells anyway: (6, 0.2) and (7, 0.2).

**Regions**
- **There is no collapse region.** The persist cells form one region of 31 of the 35 cells. It spans every `energy_cost` from 0.04 to 0.16 and every fire rate from 0 to 0.004, the default (0.10, 0.002) included.
- **The 4 collapses are all seed 3 at stream 0, and none of them are neighbours.** One is at (0.08, 0.001). The other three are at `energy_cost` 0.16 with `fire.base_rate` 0.002–0.004. Each is a grazer eat-out, at ticks 8 800–9 900, followed by the hunters starving.

**Flips**
- **All 4 flips are noise.** On streams 1–5 each cell collapses in only 1–3 of 18 replicates, spread over seeds 2 and 3.
- **No burn deaths.** Fire never becomes the dominant cause of an extinction anywhere on the grid, even at twice the default rate.

**Failures without collapse**
- Low `energy_cost` (0.04–0.06) fails `fertility_band` without any extinction. That accounts for 9 of the 13 failing cells.

**Regions**
- **There is no collapse region.** The persist cells form one region of 32 of the 36 cells.
- **All 4 collapses are seed 3 at stream 0**, and each is a grazer eat-out at ticks 8 100–13 300. They sit at `disease.grazer_rate` 0.004–0.008 and `tree.crowding_mortality` 0.02–0.05. Only two of them, (0.04, 0.004) and (0.04, 0.006), are neighbours.

- **All 4 flips are noise.** Each collapses in 2–3 of 18 replicates.

**Failures without collapse**
- Higher `crowding_mortality` and `grazer_rate` fail `fertility_band` without any extinction. That accounts for 31 of the 35 failing cells.
