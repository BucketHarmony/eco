# Viewer heartbeat

Written by `cargo heartbeat` (src/bin/heartbeat.rs), shot V13. Every file in this directory is regenerated by that one command; do not edit it by hand.

Run `../ecosim/runs/capitol-s42` at tick 10000 unless a view's flags name another, world `../ecosim/worlds/capitol`. 15 views, 1280 x 800, headless. The checks are coarse on purpose, and measured on the rows from 55% to 92% of the frame's height, below the HUD's text and above the legend: there each frame must have at least 32 colours at 5 bits a channel with no colour over 90% of it, and a view that names another must differ from it in at least 2% of those pixels by more than 8/255. There is no golden image.

The pictures show what the simulator computed, drawn by the viewer; the viewer models no ecology.
